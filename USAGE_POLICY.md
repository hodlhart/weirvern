# Weirvern Acceptable Use Policy

Weirvern is network monitoring and anomaly-detection software. It observes
network metadata (flow records) and alerts on behavioral anomalies. Like any
monitoring tool, it can be used for good or for harm. This policy states the
conditions under which the software is made available.

## 1. Authorization is mandatory

You may only run Weirvern against networks, devices, or traffic for which
**you own the network** or **hold explicit, documented authorization to
monitor**. This includes:

- Your own home/office network and devices you administer.
- Networks monitored under a written security-assessment contract, an
  employer's monitoring policy, or an educational lab environment you control.

You must not use Weirvern to collect flow data from networks you do not
own/operate or are not authorized to monitor. In many jurisdictions
(including the US under the CFAA and analogous foreign laws, and GDPR /
privacy frameworks in the EU), unauthorized traffic capture or monitoring is
a criminal or civil offense — even when the data is "just metadata."

## 2. No hostile use

You may not use Weirvern or any component of it:

- To facilitate an attack against a system or network you do not own or are
  not contracted to test.
- To build, augment, or operate surveillance of individuals without lawful
  basis (workplace surveillance of employees, tracking people without
  consent, etc.).

## 3. Data handling obligations

- Flow records can reveal behavior of people on your network (who talks to
  whom, when, how much). Treat collected flow data as personal data where
  the law requires, and:

  - store it on systems you control,
  - apply retention limits (the schema ships with TTLs; do not weaken them
    without reason),
  - do not sell or share collected flow data with third parties,
  - delete it when the lawful basis expires.

## 4. Alerts

Alerts generated by Weirvern are statistical inferences, **not proof of an
attack**. Anomaly != intrusion. Do not take punitive action against a user or
IP address on the basis of an alert alone; investigate first. The authors
accept no liability for actions taken in response to alerts (see also the
license disclaimer).

that you have read and will comply with this policy, and that you — not the
author — bear sole responsibility for how and where the software is used.
