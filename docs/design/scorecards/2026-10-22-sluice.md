visual hierarchy, recovery, and time-to-answer. The measured counts above are not opinions.

## Hard blockers

1. **State honesty = 0.** Stale gates render as "degraded"; the server's warning that the
   state is historical exists only in a hover title. The rule-empty sentence says no blocking
   rules exist while Domain Gate is on with an enabled blocklist. (`UXF-SL1`, `UXF-SL2`)
2. **A blocking rule can be confirmed after its target changes.** The first click arms the
   composer, edits do not disarm it, the arm never expires, and the consequence text is generic
   (and says "kernel drop" for a domain rule). (`UXF-SL4`)

payload and DOM give lower scores; no drop is silent:

- **entry 3 → 1:** the overview leads with rule counts and a degraded/off gate; no sentence
  answers "is enforcement working" before the fold (`UXF-SL1`, `UXF-SL5`).

  button (`UXF-SL6`).

  matches the console (`UXF-SL1`).
- **a11y 1 → 1:** unchanged; `UXF-SL7` adds the 13px mobile filter targets; `VR-KB` already
  owns keyboard-operable rule rows.
- **recovery 2 → 1:** two-step confirmation exists, but its target/blast radius is unnamed and
